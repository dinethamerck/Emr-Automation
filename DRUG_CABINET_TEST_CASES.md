# Amerck Care & Amaran EMR - Master Clinical Drug Cabinet Test Specification & Quality Matrix

## 1. Executive Summary & Clinical Quality Strategy
- **Application Under Test (AUT)**: Amerck Care EMR — Clinical Drug Cabinet & In-Clinic Formulary Management (`/admin/drug-cabinet` / `/admin/pharmacy`) & Care View Prescription Engine (`/admin/prescription`)
- **System Dual-Persona Perspective**:
  1. **Doctor / Clinical Pharmacist**: Patient safety, zero-tolerance medication error prevention, Clinical Decision Support (CDS), drug-allergy contraindications, drug-drug interaction (DDI) shielding, dosage calculations (pediatric/geriatric/renal), look-alike sound-alike (LASA) safety, controlled substances accountability, and clinical ergonomics.
  2. **Senior QA Automation Architect**: State transition validation, Boundary Value Analysis (BVA), Equivalence Partitioning (EP), race condition resilience, FEFO inventory decrements, optimistic concurrency locking, OWASP Top 10 security penetration, tamper-evident audit logging, and HIPAA / 21 CFR Part 11 compliance.
- **Target Application Scope**:
  - In-House Drug Cabinet Inventory & Master Catalog
  - Real-Time Consultation Prescription Grid (`Care View`)
  - Batch / Lot Tracking, Expiration Enforcement, and Temperature Storage (Cold-Chain)
  - Controlled Substances / High-Alert Drug Perpetual Register
  - Concurrency & Multi-Dispenser Inventory Balancing

## 12. Conclusion & Sign-Off Criteria

### Definition of Done (DoD) for Drug Cabinet Release:
1. **Zero High-Severity Clinical Safety Bugs**: No bypass of drug-allergy warnings, severe DDIs, or expired medication blocks.
2. **100% Traceability & Evidence**: All automated tests pass with video recordings (`.webm`), traces (`.zip`), screenshots (`.png`), and SHA-256 manifests.
3. **Audit Readiness**: Perpetual registers for controlled substances and stock adjustments must prove immutable non-repudiation.
4. **Clinical Ergonomics**: In-consultation drug addition from cabinet into Care View prescription grid must complete in `< 3 seconds` without UI lag.
